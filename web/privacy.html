Privacy Policy for DocuTracker

Last updated: 8 September 2026

DocuTracker ("the App") is a document-tracking application operated by the City Planning and Development Coordinator's Office (CPDCO) ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The App is intended for authorized CPDCO staff and invited users only.

Information we collect

  • Account information. A username and a password that you create. Passwords are stored only in hashed (irreversible) form.
  • Documents and images you upload. Files, photos, and document details (titles, dates, categories, notes, tracking status) that you add to the App.
  • Device notification token. A push-notification identifier issued by Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can send you reminders and status alerts.
  • Usage records. Login times, session activity, and actions performed in the App (for example, creating or editing a record), used for internal auditing and accountability.

How we use information

  • To authenticate you and keep your session secure.
  • To store, organize, and display the documents and records you manage.
  • To send operational notifications and reminders.
  • To maintain an internal activity log for office recordkeeping.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

Where data is stored

  • Supabase — hosts the account database and document records.
  • Google Drive — stores uploaded document files and images in folders controlled by CPDCO. Uploads are performed through a secure server function; the App does not access your personal Google account.
  • Google Firebase — provides push-notification delivery and application hosting.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments.

Data sharing

Information is accessible to authorized CPDCO personnel for official purposes. We may disclose information if required by law or valid legal process. Otherwise, we do not share your information outside the office.

Data retention

Account and document records are kept for as long as they are needed for office operations and applicable recordkeeping requirements. Locally cached images on your device may be cleared automatically after a period of inactivity. You may request deletion of your account (see below).

Security

We use hashed password storage, encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access-controlled storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Your choices

  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and associated records by contacting us.
  • You can disable notifications in your device settings.

Children

The App is not directed to children and is intended for office use only.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by a new "Last updated" date on this page.
